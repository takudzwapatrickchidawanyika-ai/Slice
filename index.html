The Problem

Africa Has a $130 Billion Infrastructure Crisis

Governments lack capital. Ordinary people are excluded from investing. Legacy systems are plagued by fraud, illiquidity, and no inheritance options.

01

Massive Infrastructure Gap

Africa needs $130B annually for critical infrastructure — governments simply cannot fund it alone.

02

Public Excluded

Ordinary people have no avenue to invest in the airports, hospitals, and schools being built around them.

03

No Liquidity

Traditional investors are locked in for 10-15 years with zero exit options and no price discovery.

04

Fraud & Fake Titles

Forced transfers and fraudulent title deeds erode investor trust across the continent.

05

Cash-Heavy Land Costs

Purchasing land in cash inflates capital requirements by 20-40% and crushes overall returns.

06

No Inheritance

Generational wealth cannot be seamlessly passed on or gifted to family members.

The Solution

A Four-Party Model That Works for Everyone

Governments contribute land as equity. Private capital builds. The public invests from $500. Slice Africa manages it all.

1

Government

Contributes land valued as equity — no cash required. Receives asset ownership, revenue share, and thousands of new jobs.

2

Public Investors

Invest from just $500. Earn monthly dividends, trade shares on SOBI, and pass wealth to heirs through inheritance.

3

Private Capital

Large-scale funding from M+. Access infrastructure-grade returns and diversify into high-growth African markets.

4

Slice Africa Platform

Runs the technology, wallets, and operations, and vets every project alongside the government. A fixed 10% fee — reinvested into pan-African expansion.

Slice Africa takes a fixed 10% management fee. The remaining 90% is shared proportionally by equity contribution — transparently, every month.
